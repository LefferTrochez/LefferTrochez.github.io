About

About Me

I am an Electronic Engineer and Ph.D. student in Engineering at Universidad de los Andes. My academic work is centered on robotics, control systems, modeling, simulation, embedded systems, and technology-based engineering projects.

My research interests include robotic platforms, control-oriented system integration, MATLAB/Simulink workflows, ROS 2, MuJoCo simulation, embedded electronics, sensing systems, and real-world robotic implementation.
