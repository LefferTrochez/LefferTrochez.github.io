About

About Me

I am an engineer and robotics researcher based in Bogotá, Colombia. My work focuses on the development of robotic systems that integrate modeling, simulation, control, sensing, embedded electronics, and real-world deployment.

My academic and technical interests include humanoid robotics, Sim2Real workflows, ROS 2, MATLAB/Simulink, MuJoCo simulation, embedded systems, control systems, and intelligent robotic platforms.

Current Focus

  • Sim2Real control frameworks for humanoid robots
  • ROS 2 and MuJoCo integration
  • MATLAB/Simulink-based robotic workflows
  • Embedded robotics and sensing systems
